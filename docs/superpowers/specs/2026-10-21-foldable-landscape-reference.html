Grounded in Apple's HIG: Designing for iPhone Duo calls for side toolbars/navigation in unfolded landscape, consistent state and hierarchy across displays, and minimal movement during folding. Search and shelf controls stay with the collection they affect. Recipe and brew actions occupy the side toolbar; navigation stays outside the content arrangement.

Adaptive, not device-locked: the preview frame uses your supplied 2670 × 1878 proportions, but a real layout must respond to available space, safe areas, multitasking and conditional fold/camera reserved regions. A fixed percentage split or this schematic toolbar is not a substitute for those APIs. Native guidance is verified; Expo/React Native support is not yet assessed.

Unfolded only: no tabletop reorganisation is decided here. The graph and ladder are a natural content pair to adapt around reserved regions, without changing the task or sending brew commands.
