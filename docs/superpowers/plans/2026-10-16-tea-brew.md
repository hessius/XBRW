### Tool API, alignment and limitations

`analyseCapture(brewText, [{name, text}, ...], {logDate?})` is also exported for
local tests. The CLI requires one `--brew`, one or more `--log` arguments and
optionally `--log-date YYYY-MM-DD`; errors are explicit on stderr with exit 1.
It validates the version-1 export's numeric clocks, ordered samples and present
plan stages. Missing/zero `pouringAt` leaves the sample origin unknown; it never
silently substitutes `startedAt`. Missing plans and empty sample streams are
reported as gaps, not fabricated observations.

App `frameLogText` uses **UTC time of day with no date**. `--log-date` is the UTC
date of the **first timed frame line** in a copied session, or the first timed
line in a stored/raw log, not the display's local “When” value. The copied
session puts its separate connection history before its frame history: connection
lines stay explicitly undated rather than causing false backwards-clock or
midnight interpretations. Supply both files together only when their first frame
date is the same; otherwise analyze each separately with its own date. Without
a supplied date, raw times remain visible but logs cannot align to epoch
brew/sample times.
A backward jump larger than twelve hours is treated as a midnight rollover
with a warning, not proof of continuity; smaller backwards jumps leave that
line and subsequent lines unaligned. Missing whole days cannot be recovered
from this format.

The report includes the plan, sample span relative to `startedAt`/`pouringAt`,
raw sample indices with counts/channel minima/maxima/last readings, and an
aligned timeline of every sample and every original log line. Untimed headers,
metadata, blanks and malformed timing are retained with source/line numbers;
unaligned rows sort after aligned rows without losing their original positions.
Overlapping session/stored lines are not deduplicated. Sent command numbers
are extracted from type-1 frame headers only (uploads 4513/8001/8004, execute
4512/8002); CRC, receipt success and actual execution are **not** validated.
Command counts cover all supplied lines, not necessarily the target brew;
unaligned/out-of-record commands get warnings.

Missing uploads/execute or an aligned frame tail beyond `endedAt` get coverage
warnings. Even their presence never proves complete coverage: retained histories
are bounded, exclude weight streams, and stored frames stop when the recorder
writes. A tail frame can belong to other activity. No tea phase boundary,
completion, root cause or trustworthy release amount is inferred, and an
unobserved planned index is not proof the physical stage never ran.

## Physical capture and observation-schema addendum — 2026-10-16

The user later supplied a complete, physically observed three-steep capture.
The anonymized timeline and water-only landmarks are recorded in
`docs/machine-integration/ble-protocol.md` and replayed from
`library/machine/__tests__/fixtures/teaCapture.ts`. Provenance is limited to
firmware `V12.0D.500`; the machine model and app build/source for the brew were
not established. The cup was replaced during the brew, so its readings and any
cup-derived rates are not evidence. The independent water stream is valid.

The capture supports these observation boundaries for this recorded sequence:
`40510` values `0`, `1`, `2` start the three cycles; `9012` followed by state
`0x45` marks the fill/steep boundary; state `0x23` follows each observed steep;
the next `40510`, or final `40511`, closes the combined release/drain window.
Steep durations from `0x45` to `0x23` were 44.627, 59.326 and 89.628 seconds.
The stream shows measured fill and subsequent water, but does not identify a
separate physical release-versus-drain boundary. It gives no evidence about
the earlier truncated recording's cause.

Before changing the recorder, the intended retention-safe record shape is:
optional `teaCycles`, one compact entry per observed cycle, aligned to the
zero-based `40510` index and containing only optional sample-clock millisecond
markers (`fillStartedAt`, `fillEndedAt`, `steepStartedAt`, `steepEndedAt`,
`endingEndedAt`) and optional measured millilitres (`fillMl`,
`releaseWaterMl`). A missing marker or amount stays absent; it is never stored
as zero. The record's existing outcome, failure and
pause intervals remain authoritative for interruption and manual-pause context.
Cycle phase and completion copy are derived from those observations plus the
snapshotted `beverageKind`; no stage-index inference is made for legacy records.
This addendum authorizes observation and presentation only: it does not
authorize a tea encoding change or a claim that the original truncation defect
has been fixed.
